# JSXMotion

**Narrated videos written like apps.** Ask Claude Code for a video and get a finished mp4: your app's screens rebuilt in JSX, a natural ElevenLabs voice, every tap landing on the spoken word.

[![Every tap lands on its spoken word](https://github.com/VisroliyaHimanshu/jsxmotion/releases/download/demo-video/jsxmotion-tap.gif)](https://github.com/VisroliyaHimanshu/jsxmotion/releases/tag/demo-video)

**▶ [Download the 30 second how to video (mp4, with sound)](https://github.com/VisroliyaHimanshu/jsxmotion/releases/download/demo-video/jsxmotion-how-to.mp4)**, made with JSXMotion itself.

## Get started

1. Install, inside Claude Code: `/plugin install jsxmotion --marketplace VisroliyaHimanshu/jsxmotion`
2. Paste your [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) API key when Claude Code asks, then **Save configuration**.
3. Ask: *"Make a 30 second how to video for our sign in flow."*

Claude asks a few questions, shows you the script, and spends credits only after you approve it.

**[How to use it, step by step →](HOW-TO-USE.md)**

## How it works

<p align="center"><img src="docs/how-it-works.jpg" width="900" alt="How JSXMotion makes a video, in three stages. 1, Plan: you ask for a video, Claude asks a few questions, writes a script with every beat tied to a word, and you approve it before credits are spent. 2, Sound: your voice or one picked from free previews, ElevenLabs records one take, every clip is checked by transcription, then pacing is tightened and music and effects are added. 3, Picture: screens are rebuilt in JSX timed to the words, with camera, depth and motion, every tap is checked on its frame, and you get your mp4 and a short report."></p>
